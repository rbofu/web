Who We Are

Our Mandate

Leading Tanzania's response to neglected tropical diseases

The Neglected Tropical Diseases Control Programme (NTDCP) sits within the Ministry of Health and coordinates the planning, financing, and delivery of NTD control activities across mainland Tanzania, in partnership with regional and district health management teams.

We work across the full disease control cycle: mapping and surveillance, mass drug administration, morbidity management and disability prevention, and monitoring progress toward WHO elimination targets.

Mission & Vision

Vision: A Tanzania free from the burden of neglected tropical diseases.

Mission: To reduce and eliminate the public health impact of NTDs through equitable, evidence-based, and sustainable interventions delivered in partnership with communities.

Our Journey

Our History

2006

Programme established

NTDCP was formed to integrate previously separate disease-specific control efforts under one national programme.

2012

National master plan launched

Tanzania adopted its first integrated NTD master plan, aligning treatment schedules across diseases and regions.

2020–present

Elimination targets in reach

Several regions have reached WHO elimination thresholds for trachoma and lymphatic filariasis, with validation surveys ongoing.

How We Work

Programme Structure

NTDCP operates through a national coordination unit, regional NTD focal persons, and district-level implementers who work alongside community drug distributors and school teachers to deliver treatment and track progress. Technical committees on each disease area guide strategy and review evidence to adjust intervention plans annually.
