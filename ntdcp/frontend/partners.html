Our Partners

NTDCP's work is made possible through the support of technical, financial, and implementing partners working alongside the Ministry of Health.

World Health Organization

Technical guidance, disease verification, and access to donated medicines.

RTI International

Programmatic and technical assistance across mapping, MDA, and surveillance.

Sightsavers

Support for trachoma and onchocerciasis elimination activities.

END Fund

Financing for treatment delivery in underserved districts.

Ifakara Health Institute

Operational research and impact evaluation partnership.

U.S. CDC

Laboratory capacity strengthening and surveillance systems support.
